The base view shows the name and gift chance. Turn on Statistics view for the !, opens and rules-out numbers (gift + ! + opens always add up to 100). Hover a tile (tap and hold on a phone) for the same numbers in words.

  1. Tap the two ✕ tiles from your game (never on the edge).
  2. Set-up: copy any tiles already open in your game by tapping them here: 1× opened, 2× !, 3× gift, 4× hidden again. New game? Skip it. Then press Start coaching. You can come back any time with Set up board.
  3. Press Advisor: it plays every possible gift layout to the end and turns the click that finishes the board in the fewest clicks on average green. Usually that's the highest chance, but not always. Several green tiles are equally good. Click it in your game.
  4. Tap the same tile here and pick what your game showed: Gift, ! or Opened. Each button shows how likely that result was.
  5. Gift found: the ! tiles next to it flash as !?. Say whether each still shows ! or turned plain.
  6. Wrong entry? Undo (Ctrl+Z) and Redo (Ctrl+Y) step through every entry, including set-up taps.
  7. Practice (top switch): a random board that answers by itself. On a phone, the first tap shows the possibilities, the second opens the tile.

For the curious: how the Advisor decides, and how its results were measured.

Game model

  • 42 tiles in rows of 8-9-8-9-8. Two ✕ tiles start open, on any 2 of the 20 inner tiles. 3 rewards on any 3 of the other 40 tiles, uniformly at random.
  • A click shows a reward, a ! (a hidden reward is next to it), or opens the tile and its hidden neighbours. Opened neighbours tell you nothing.
  • A ! goes plain once every reward next to it is found.

How the Advisor picks

  • It lists every reward layout that still fits the board (9,880 at the start).
  • For every tile you could click, it follows every one of those layouts to the end of the game, with a strong base strategy playing the later clicks (highest chance, ties to the click that leaves the fewest layouts). That gives the exact expected number of clicks to finish.
  • It picks the tile with the lowest number. This is a one-step policy improvement, so on average it can never be worse than the base strategy.
  • The best first click for all 190 ✕ pairs is precomputed, so the first hint is instant. Everything else is calculated in your browser.

Every board: 190 ✕ pairs × 9,880 reward layouts = 1,877,200 boards. Each strategy's full game tree was evaluated on all of them, so these numbers are exact.

Where the gain comes from

  • First click: 0.03 clicks saved per board
  • Before the 1st reward: 0.17
  • Between the 1st and 2nd: 0.09
  • Last reward: 0.009. Exact perfect play for the last reward would save 0.0098, so the endgame is essentially solved.

Checks

  • The in-browser engine was compared against an independent simulator on 113 positions: 0 differences in picks or values.
  • Full games played automatically through this page in a browser, with no errors.

Limitations

  • 10.08 is not proven optimal. True perfect play is at or below it, most likely only slightly.
  • All numbers assume the game places ✕ tiles and rewards uniformly at random. If it doesn't, the numbers shift.
