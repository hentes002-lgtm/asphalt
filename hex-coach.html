How to use

  1. Set the two ✕ tiles first. Tap the two tiles that show ✕ in your game. They are never on the edge.
  2. Game already in progress? Still in set-up, tap each opened tile: once for opened (dark), twice for !, three times for gift, four times to hide it again.
  3. Press Start coaching, then Advisor. Click the green tile in your game.
  4. Tap the same tile here and choose what it showed: Gift, !, or Opened. Opened also opens its hidden neighbours here, the same as in your game.
  5. Gift found: the ! tiles next to it flash as !?. Tap each one and say whether your game still shows ! or a plain tile. A ! disappears once every gift around it is found.
  6. Wrong entry? Press Undo.
  7. Random game: practise here without another game. The tiles answer by themselves; use Advisor whenever you want a hint.
