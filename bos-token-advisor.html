How it works
  • Each 10-pull: pulls 1–9 hit a featured car at the drop chance; pull 10 always hits, in both the BOS and the Bolide packs.
  • A hit is 4 blueprints (BOS packs) or 3 (Bolide pack) for one of the pack's cars at random, or 15 when "15-BP drops" is on.
  • A car that maxes mid 10-pull stays in the pool until that 10-pull ends (extra hits become Trade Coins). After that, hits only go to cars still short of max.
  • Key cars collect blueprints without the key; the key itself isn't modelled.
  • Typical: half of 6,000 simulated runs finish within it. Safe: 9 in 10 do.
  • The 15-BP rate isn't published; 0.4% is what the community figure of 11.33 BP per 10-pull implies. The Bolide pack's 10% default is the wiki's figure for car-specific packs.
  • Share links carry your garage in the address (?code= or #code-). Opening one loads that garage without touching your saved one until you tap Save.
  • Car data: ASEC, asphalt9.info, Asphalt Wiki. Drop chance and prices are player-reported. Fan-made, not affiliated with Gameloft.
